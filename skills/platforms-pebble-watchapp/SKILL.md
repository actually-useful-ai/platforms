# Platforms Pebble Watchapp

Deliver interactive Pebble apps (not faces).

## Guidance
- Structure app keys, menus, and messages for small screens and limited buttons.
- Validate build, install, drive, screenshot, and log inspection before store staging.
- Package `.pbw` only after validation evidence exists.
- Do not copy no-license third-party skill prose; follow current rePebble docs.
