# Platforms Fire TV

Leanback Fire TV packaging.

## Required checks
- `LEANBACK_LAUNCHER` (or equivalent) declared
- D-pad focus and Back behavior
- No touch-only primary flows
- Keep-awake / screensaver policy explicit
- APK contents and version codes reproducible

Submission to Amazon remains a human step after store-staged artifacts exist.
