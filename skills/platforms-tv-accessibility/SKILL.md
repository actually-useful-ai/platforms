# Platforms TV Accessibility

Review television and leanback experiences for remote and assistive use.

## Checks
- D-pad focus order and visible focus
- Back/Exit behavior
- Screen reader / TTS labels where the platform supports them
- Captions and audio descriptions when media exists
- Reduced motion and readable contrast
- No touch-only affordances on TV targets
- Screensaver / always-on policy documented

Record findings; do not mark device-tested without a real device or emulator run.
